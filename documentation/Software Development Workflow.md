### Optimizing AI-Assisted Software Development Workflow

Here's a balanced workflow for developing software features that leverages AI capabilities efficiently while maintaining developer control and minimizing credit usage:

#### 1. Planning Phase: Strategic AI Usage

- **Feature Definition Workshop**: Begin with human-driven brainstorming to define feature requirements, constraints, and success criteria.
- **Architecture Planning**: Use AI to generate high-level architectural diagrams (like PlantUML) and explore design alternatives based on your requirements.
- **Task Breakdown**: Have AI help decompose features into discrete, manageable tasks with clear dependencies.

#### 2. Design Phase: Collaborative Approach

- **Interface-First Design**: Define interfaces and contracts manually to maintain control over system boundaries.
- **AI Design Review**: Use AI to review your interfaces for completeness, consistency, and potential edge cases.
- **Pattern Suggestion**: Ask AI to recommend appropriate design patterns for specific components rather than designing entire systems.

#### 3. Implementation Phase: Targeted AI Assistance

- **Scaffolding Generation**: Use AI to generate initial code scaffolding based on your interfaces and design.
- **Complex Algorithm Assistance**: Reserve AI credits for complex algorithms, data structures, or performance-critical sections.
- **Batch Processing**: Collect multiple related coding questions and submit them together rather than in separate sessions.
- **Template Creation**: Have AI help create reusable templates for common patterns in your codebase.

#### 4. Testing Phase: Balanced Approach

- **Test Case Identification**: Use AI to identify comprehensive test scenarios and edge cases.
- **Test Scaffolding**: Generate test scaffolding with AI, then manually implement the test logic.
- **Selective Test Generation**: Use AI to generate tests for complex or critical components only.

#### 5. Documentation Phase: Efficient AI Usage

- **Documentation Templates**: Create templates for documentation that AI can fill in based on code analysis.
- **Targeted Documentation**: Focus AI on documenting complex components rather than simple, self-explanatory code.
- **Batch Documentation**: Process multiple documentation tasks in a single AI session.

#### 6. Credit-Saving Strategies

- **Local AI Models**: Use smaller, local AI models for simpler tasks to save credits on cloud-based services.
- **Caching AI Responses**: Store and reuse AI-generated solutions for common patterns.
- **Prompt Libraries**: Maintain a library of effective prompts for common development tasks.
- **Session Planning**: Plan AI sessions in advance to maximize value per session.
- **Incremental Refinement**: Start with minimal prompts and refine gradually rather than requesting complete solutions immediately.

#### 7. Maintaining Developer Control

- **Review-First Workflow**: Always review AI-generated code before integration.
- **Architectural Boundaries**: Establish clear boundaries that AI cannot modify without explicit approval.
- **Style Enforcement**: Run AI-generated code through linters and style checkers to maintain consistency.
- **Knowledge Transfer**: Document AI-generated solutions with explanations to build team knowledge.
- **Progressive Delegation**: Start with small, well-defined tasks for AI and gradually increase complexity as confidence grows.

#### 8. Feedback Loop Integration

- **Solution Evaluation**: Systematically evaluate the quality of AI-generated solutions to identify areas for improvement.
- **Prompt Refinement**: Continuously refine prompts based on the quality of AI responses.
- **Knowledge Capture**: Document successful AI interactions for future reference.

#### 9. Hybrid Development Model

- **Pair Programming with AI**: Use AI as a pair programming partner for specific tasks.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Automation**: Automate repetitive tasks with AI but keep humans in the review loop.
- **Expertise Augmentation**: Use AI to supplement team expertise in specialized domains.

#### 10. Continuous Improvement

- **AI Capability Tracking**: Stay informed about new AI capabilities relevant to your development process.
- **Workflow Refinement**: Regularly review and refine your AI integration workflow.
- **Team Training**: Ensure all team members understand how to effectively leverage AI in their work.

By following this balanced approach, you can maximize the benefits of AI assistance while maintaining control over your development process and optimizing credit usage for the highest-value activities.
